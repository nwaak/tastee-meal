description: "Long-grain rice toasted until golden, then simmered with onion, tomato sauce, and chicken broth with garlic salt and cumin."
prep_time: "10 min"
cook_time: "25 mins"
total_time: "35 min"
servings: "6"
image: "https://unsplash.com/photos/a-close-up-of-a-spoon-in-a-bowl-of-food-MR4747QM9zM"

ingredient_sections:
  - section_title: "Rice"
    items:
      - "3 tbsp vegetable oil"
      - "1 cup uncooked long-grain rice"
      - "1 tsp garlic salt (or 1/4 tsp garlic, 3/4 tsp salt)"
      - "1/2 tsp ground cumin"
      - "1/4 cup chopped onion"
      - "1/2 cup tomato sauce"
      - "2 cups chicken broth"

instructions:
  - title: "Toast the Rice"
    text: "Heat the oil and add the rice. Cook until puffed and golden. Sprinkle with the garlic salt and cumin."
  - title: "Add Onion"
    text: "Stir in the onion and cook until tender."
  - title: "Simmer"
    text: "Add the tomato sauce and chicken broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat. Cover and simmer for 20-25 minutes."

notes: "I prefer to use Basmati rice"
