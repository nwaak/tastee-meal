Lemon Herb Chicken & Quinoa Bowl

Pan-seared chicken breast marinated in garlic, lemon zest, and oregano over fluffy quinoa with roasted vegetables.

Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Total Time 35 mins
Servings 4 bowls

Bowl Base

Instructions

1
Marinate the Chicken

Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and black pepper in a bowl. Coat the chicken breasts evenly and let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes while starting the quinoa.

2
Cook the Quinoa

Combine rinsed quinoa and broth in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until liquid is absorbed. Remove from heat and fluff with a fork.

3
Sear the Chicken

Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for 5–6 minutes per side until golden brown and internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C). Transfer to a cutting board and rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

4
Assemble Bowls

Divide quinoa among four bowls. Top with sliced chicken, cherry tomatoes, and diced cucumber. Drizzle any remaining pan juices over top.

Kitchen Notes

Keeps well in airtight glass containers for up to 4 days. Store the cucumbers and tomatoes separately if packing for lunch so they stay crisp when reheating the chicken and quinoa.
